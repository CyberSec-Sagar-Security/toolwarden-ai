## Assessment

Spot-checked against the 4 records above (2026-08-11):

- **DeBERTa attention:** partially useful, not crisp. On the InjecAgent injection example, top tokens included `Ignore`, `instructions`, `Bitcoin`, `sell` — genuinely part of the injected span — but also `slim`, `laptop`, `4`, which are from the surrounding benign product description, not the injection. Roughly half the top-8 tokens were actually injection-relevant. For the AgentDojo injection example the whole text *is* the injected instruction, so there's no irrelevant content to mis-attend to — that case isn't a meaningful localization test either way.
- **LightGBM SHAP:** leans on coarse surface features (`text_length`, `topic_shift_score`) far more than the semantically-targeted ones (`imperative_phrasing_score`, `jailbreak_signature_count`) in 3 of the 4 cases — including the clearest injection example, where `imperative_phrasing_score`'s SHAP contribution (0.05) was two orders of magnitude smaller than `text_length`'s (7.69). The engineered features aren't wrong, but the model leans on cruder correlates of them more than on the interpretable signal itself.
- **Conclusion:** both explainers produce real signal, not noise, but neither cleanly isolates the injected span the way a human reviewer would want. Treat this as directional evidence for a human approver (Phase 6 approval queue), not a precise highlight — don't oversell this in any writeup.
